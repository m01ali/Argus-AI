Why stateless discovery

Stages 1 & 4 use stateless single-shot prompts — the ShellGPT --shell isolation property the thesis identifies as the mechanism enabling reliable security-sensitive command generation, free of conversational guardrail amplification.

Why validation gates everything

An unverifiable finding never reaches Patch Proposal. A fabricated scan result therefore cannot drive a fabricated patch — validation converts the hallucination risk into a contained, auditable checkpoint.

Why it’s privacy-first

Aardvark and Claude Security upload source code and scan output to cloud inference. Argus-AI runs the equivalent reasoning entirely on local LLMs, making it deployable in regulated environments where the cloud tools are categorically prohibited.
