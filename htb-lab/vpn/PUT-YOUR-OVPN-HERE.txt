Put your HackTheBox .ovpn file in this folder.

1. Log in to https://www.hackthebox.com  (MonitorsFour is a RETIRED machine, so
   you need a VIP / VIP+ subscription to spawn it).
2. Top-right "Connect to HTB" -> choose the Machines/Labs VPN region -> download
   the .ovpn (e.g. lab_<username>.ovpn).
3. Drop it in this folder, then import it into OpenVPN Connect (see ../README.md
   section 3).

This folder is gitignored for *.ovpn — your VPN key never gets committed.
